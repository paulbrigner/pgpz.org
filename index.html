About PGPZ

An independent policy organization for the Zcash ecosystem.

PGPZ supports broader understanding and responsible adoption of Zcash by translating technical systems into useful policy context and organizing informed, credible engagement.

Privacy needs a practical voice in public policy.

PGPZ brings together education, community participation, policy analysis, and advocacy so people can engage with shared context while preserving distinct roles and perspectives.

The aim is straightforward: help decision-makers understand what privacy-preserving digital cash does, why it matters, and how thoughtful policy can protect both civil liberties and legitimate public interests.

Michele Korver is the Head of Regulatory at a16z crypto where she helps a16z's portfolio companies to navigate the regulatory landscape and works to educate and liaise with government agencies and policymakers.

Financial privacy

Privacy keeps digital finance safe, practical, and trustworthy.

Financial privacy helps people and businesses protect themselves from theft, harassment, discrimination, and unwanted surveillance. As finance becomes more digital, exposed transaction data can reveal spending, income, location, and relationships at scale.

  • 01 Personal safetyA donor may want to support a political cause without creating a public target.
  • 02 Sensitive needsA patient may need medical supplies without exposing private health information.
  • 03 Business confidentialityAn organization may need to protect vendor payments, payroll, and strategy.

What PGPZ does

A coordinated policy home for Zcash.

The work connects stakeholder education, global advocacy, civil liberties, policy response, and developer protections.

  1. 01 Stakeholder education

    Give policymakers, regulators, and industry stakeholders clear, useful context about Zcash.

  2. 02 Global coordination

    Connect policy work across jurisdictions while respecting local knowledge, needs, and strategy.

  3. 03 Civil liberties

    Advance the public-interest case for privacy-preserving infrastructure as digital finance expands.

  4. 04 Policy response

    Help the ecosystem understand and respond constructively to policy affecting privacy-preserving networks.

  5. 05 Developer protections

    Support clear rules, due process, and appropriate safe harbors for people who build and maintain non-custodial privacy software.

About Zcash

Digital money with strong privacy protections.

Zcash is a decentralized digital currency that uses zero-knowledge cryptography to support shielded transactions. The network can verify a valid transfer without publicly revealing the sender, recipient, or amount.

Fixed supply and a decentralized network
Shielded transfers using zero-knowledge proofs
Selective disclosure when context requires it
